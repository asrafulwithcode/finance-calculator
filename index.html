How Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Works

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest small amounts periodically into mutual funds. By compounding returns over long durations, even modest monthly installments generate significant wealth. The formula uses compound interest calculated monthly over the tenure.

Understanding Equated Monthly Installments (EMI)

An EMI is a fixed monthly payment made by a borrower to a lender. It comprises both principal repayments and accrued interest. Knowing your EMI in advance ensures optimal cash flow management.
